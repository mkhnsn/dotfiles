# Patterns

Templates for text that comes up in every app. Fill the brackets; drop what
doesn't apply.

## Errors

`Couldn't [action]. [What to do].`

- "Couldn't save the run. Reload to see the latest."
- "Couldn't reach the server. Your changes are saved here and sync when you're back online."

With the cause from the system: `Couldn't [action]: [reason]`. Only when the
reason helps the person act.

## Validation

Say what's needed, beside the field: "Use a dot for decimals (0.45)." Not
"Invalid input".

## Confirmations

`[Verb] "[name]" [and what goes with it] [for whom]? [Can it be undone].`

- "Delete "Shop" and its history for everyone in "Makerspace"? This can't be undone."
- "Leave "Makerspace"? You keep copies of its libraries, without later changes."

The button repeats the verb: "Delete library", not "OK".

## Success

Past tense, the outcome, and where it went if that isn't obvious:
"Saved. Applied to Core One 0.4." Skip it when the change is visible on
screen.

## Empty states

`No [things] yet.` plus one button that makes the first one.

## Notices and warnings

One sentence on what's true, one on what to do: "This browser can't save
libraries. Export a zip to keep your work."

## Limits

`[Who] has reached its limit of [limits]. [How to make room].`

## Toggles for detail

Summary names what's inside: "What's shared", "How this works", "Options".
Not "Learn more" or "Details".
