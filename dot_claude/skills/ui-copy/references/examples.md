# Before and after

Real rewrites, grouped by what was wrong. From spoolbook (filament
profiles for PrusaSlicer), where the guide started.

## Explains the mechanism

| Before                                                                                                                                                                                | After                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Each preset carries its Prusa parent's full settings, taken from Prusa Research's system profiles: the ones your PrusaSlicer version installs, from the preset repository PrusaSlicer itself uses. | Presets are built on Prusa's system profiles for this PrusaSlicer version. |

## Narrates, and chains clauses

| Before                                                                                                                                                                                                | After                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Someone shared this filament's profiles with you. Download the PrusaSlicer presets below, or copy it into your own libraries to calibrate on from there; your copy is yours, and later changes here don't reach it. | Read-only. Download the presets, or copy it to your libraries to calibrate it. Later changes here won't affect your copy. |

## Hedges and justifies

| Before                                                                                                                                                | After                                                                 |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| This test is meant to come later: do Filament diameter first. You can still record it, but its result rests on settings that aren't calibrated yet. | Do ⟨Filament diameter⟩ first. Results recorded now may be inaccurate. |

## Edge cases up front

| Before                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | After                                                                                                                         |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Anyone with a link sees this library, or one filament's profiles, as it is in the cloud now, and can download the presets… No account needed. That includes run notes and spool details; photos and data sheets only if you include them. … | Anyone with a link can view this library or filament and download its presets. No account needed. (The rest goes behind a "What's shared" toggle.) |

## Paragraph where a sentence will do

| Before                                                                                                                                  | After                                                              |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| This browser isn't letting spoolbook save, so libraries last only until you close or reload the page. Export a zip to keep your work. | This browser can't save libraries. Export a zip to keep your work. |

## Cut too far (and fixed)

| Too short                                      | Right                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| You left the team that had "Shop".             | You no longer have access to the team that had "Shop". (Owners can remove members.) |
| Deleting can't be undone.                      | Deleting can't be undone. A team library is deleted for every member.     |
| Results recorded now may be off.               | Results recorded now may be inaccurate.                                   |
