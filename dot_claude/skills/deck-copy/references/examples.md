# Before and after

Rewrites grouped by what was wrong. From the Anima Puck pitch deck
(unstable-studios/anima, `hardware/puck/marketing/deck/`), where the guide
started.

## Slogan headline

| Before                                                       | After                                                    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Building is free. Premium sells the library, not the lights. | Everything needed to build and run a show is free        |
| Cues, not frames                                             | Pucks send small cues and draw their own lights          |

The first slide's body then lists what Premium adds: the cloud library,
timeline choreography, music sync and fleet tools, at $3–6/mo.

## Importance word instead of a fact

| Before                                           | After                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Sync is the differentiator.                      | No shipping sealed product we found syncs without a router. WLED has an experimental ESP-NOW sync.     |
| Both prove people pay for this shape of product. | Blueghozt sells for $110–130 and SwitchPros for $659, both with free apps.                             |

## Colon reveal

| Before                                                                         | After                                                               |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The closest analogs aren't in cosplay at all: they're in car mods and offroad. | The closest analogs are car-lighting and offroad-power controllers. |

## Topic label instead of a claim

| Before            | After                                                     |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Market validation | Car builders already pay $110–132 for an app-set light controller |
| Business model    | Hardware margin funds the business                        |

The business-model slide's body then shows the three revenue lines:
hardware, accessories and the app library.
