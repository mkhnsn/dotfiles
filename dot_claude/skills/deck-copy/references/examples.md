# Before and after

Rewrites grouped by what was wrong. From the Anima Puck pitch deck
(unstable-studios/anima, `hardware/puck/marketing/deck/`), where the guide
started.

## Slogan headline

| Before                                                   | After                                                                  |
| -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Building is free. Premium sells the library, not the lights. | Every build feature is free. Premium is the cloud show library, at $3–6/mo. |
| Cues, not frames                                         | Pucks send small cues and draw their own lights                         |

## Importance word instead of a fact

| Before                                   | After                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sync is the differentiator.              | No sealed product we found syncs units without a router. WLED's ESP-NOW sync is in beta.   |
| Both prove people pay for this shape of product. | Blueghozt sells for $110–130 and SwitchPros for $659, both with free apps.          |

## Colon reveal

| Before                                                                           | After                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| The closest analogs aren't in cosplay at all: they're in car mods and offroad.   | The closest analogs are car-lighting and offroad-power controllers.                            |

## Topic label instead of a claim

| Before            | After                                                       |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------- |
| Market validation | Car builders already pay $110–130 for a sealed app controller |
| Business model    | Hardware pays for the business; accessories add margin       |

## Notes written as a second slide

| Before                                                                                       | After                                                                                                              |
